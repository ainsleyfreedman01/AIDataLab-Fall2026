Which PII terms does each district's AI policy name?

54 of 110 districts with privacy passages name at least one specific kind of personal information. Rows are districts, ordered by how many terms they name; columns are terms, ordered by how many districts name them. Click any blue cell to see the exact words in the policy, and where they are.

How each cell was decided
  • Source text: the privacy passages in Privacy Passages/*.pdf, quoted from each district's own documents. A district with several documents is one row; a term counts if any of its documents names it.
  • A term is searched for only in text near PII, confidential-data, or "do not enter/share" language, so a word used for another reason elsewhere in a policy doesn't count.
  • Every match was read. 251 cells come from pattern matches confirmed by reading; 1 were added by hand because the wording had no pattern. 41 matches (district and state documents) were rejected; each is listed below with its reason.
  • "Named" means the policy lists it as personal or confidential data to protect, including lists labelled "confidential" or "sensitive" and descriptions of laws (e.g. FERPA protecting education records).
