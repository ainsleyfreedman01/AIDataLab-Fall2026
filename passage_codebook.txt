Student privacy passage pilot: codebook v1

Research question
How explicitly do the reviewed K-12 AI excerpts address secondary uses and
retention of student data, and what safeguards do they describe?

Scope
Model training/improvement; disclosure to vendors or other third parties and
secondary uses; retention periods, deletion triggers, and deletion rights.
An initial upload restriction alone is outside the secondary-use category.
Generic security, storage location, access control, curriculum objectives,
and staff professional development do not establish these protections.
Do not infer provisions from legal citations alone.

Units
Retrieval and retrieval evaluation use extracted paragraph-by-category pairs.
A paragraph can contain multiple provisions and categories. Page breaks can
split a paragraph; adjacent context must be checked. Retrieval recall is NOT
provision-level recall or an estimate of completeness of original policies.
Substantive coding uses individual provisions, with separate rows for distinct
rules or exceptions even if they occur in the same retrieved paragraph.
Count a document once per protection for document-level summaries.

Relevance labels
yes: explicit student/personal-data handling or evaluation of a tool's relevant
data practices in the applicable educational context.
no: no relevant content, or a lexical match outside the research scope.
uncertain: data connection, referent, or context is insufficient for a firm code.
Exclude uncertain labels from primary precision/recall denominators; report them.
Generic "avoid advertisements" is uncertain unless use of student information
for advertising is established. Generic storage is not retention.
Curriculum statements about training models are not vendor restrictions.
Disclosure incident response alone is not a secondary-use authorization rule.

Categories and subcodes
model_training: training, fine-tuning, or improvement using student/personal data.
Subcodes: training_reuse; service_improvement.
sharing_secondary_use: explicit disclosure or downstream sharing of student data;
sale, advertising, profiling, and purpose limitations on data use.
Subcodes: disclosure; sale; advertising; profiling; purpose_limitation.
retention_deletion: duration of keeping data; when removal occurs; deletion
procedures or requests. Not storage geography, encryption, or accidental loss.
Subcodes: retention_limit; deletion_trigger; deletion_request; deletion_procedure.
Allow multiple subcodes. A warning or vetting question is relevant but is not
automatically an established requirement.

Substantive fields (never inferred by the keyword finder)
treatment: restricts; conditional_permission; permits; discussion_or_review; unclear.
force: mandatory_wording; recommendation; descriptive; unclear.
conditions: literal qualifying language, not a standardized assumption.
actor: named actor in the text, or unspecified.
source_function: operative_text; guidance; template; curriculum; resource_summary;
unknown. Function may differ by section within one document.
authority: record document-level authority separately; source unverified until
checked against an original source. Mandatory wording is not enforceability.
Record a quotation, PDF excerpt page, original-source label, and ambiguity notes.
Do not flatten mixed provisions into a single strongest judgment.

Quality protocol
Ten sampled PDFs, seed 20261016: first five development, last five validation.
The sample is random and not stratified; verify its scope before generalizing.
Freeze rules before examining validation text. Any subsequent changes require
a new version and a fresh held-out sample before claiming new validation.
Read every sampled excerpt without consulting hit flags to assign reference
labels. This assistant's review is NOT independent human annotation.
Evaluate category-specific precision/recall against the full-read reference.
Explicitly inspect no-hit cases and report zero-positive categories as undefined
recall, not perfect recall. Human review remains required for candidate passages.
Second-person independent review, visual PDF checks, and original-source checks
remain required before poster-ready claims.
