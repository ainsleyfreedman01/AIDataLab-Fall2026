Respect, Privacy, and Data Security
Source: page 4

Users must follow district policies on privacy and data security when interacting with AI tools.
Personal and student data must be protected, and AI should not be used in a way that violates
privacy laws or district guidelines. Teachers are responsible for educating students about the
importance of protecting their data.

Safety, Privacy and Security
Source: page 5

AI Safety and Compliance
The District complies with all relevant safety, privacy, and security regulations regarding the use of
technology, including federal laws FERPA and COPPA, to protect student data and privacy. The
district will work closely with AI vendors and tool providers to ensure that all AI applications adhere
to strict safety, privacy, and security standards. Contracts with AI vendors will include clear
requirements for compliance with legal regulations, as well as ongoing monitoring to ensure
continued adherence. These measures will ensure that all AI tools used within the district meet the
highest standards of security and data protection.

Board policy CQ Legal and Local guide our use of technology and resources.

Data Guidelines for AI Technology
To protect district information and student data, regulated, confidential, and sensitive data must not
be input into AI technology that is not listed on p.9 of the handbook. Examples of this data include:

• Regulated Data - Health information, credit card information; and criminal justice information
• Confidential Data - Personally Identifiable Information (PII), Intellectual property
• Sensitive Data - Operational information, Personnel records, internal communications
A full listing of these data types can be found in CQ(REG).

Ethical Use and Transparency
The District will ensure ethical AI practices and transparency in education by implementing clear
policies that protect student privacy, ensure data security, and promote fairness. By complying with
legal standards, conducting regular audits, and checking for bias, we will safeguard responsible AI
use. Transparency will be maintained by openly communicating how AI tools work, the data they
collect, and their decision-making processes.

Glossary of AI Terms (privacy-related excerpts)
Source: pages 11–12

• Consent Management: The process of obtaining, tracking, and managing user consent,
  especially in compliance with laws like FERPA and COPPA.
• COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act): A federal law that imposes requirements on
  websites and online services to protect the privacy of children under 13 years of age.
• Data Encryption: The process of converting data into a coded format to prevent unauthorized
  access during storage or transmission.
• Data Privacy: The practice of handling and protecting personal data to ensure it is only used for
  its intended purpose and safeguarded from unauthorized access.
• Digital Citizenship: The responsible use of technology and the internet by students and
  educators, encompassing ethical behavior, privacy, and respect for others online.
• FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act): A federal law that protects the privacy of
  student education records and gives parents certain rights regarding their children's education
  data.
