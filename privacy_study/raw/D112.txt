Excerpted verbatim from Artificial Intelligence (AI) Integration Framework for School Districts (Michigan Virtual). Page
numbers refer to the PDF pages of the source file. This is a one-page rubric table with three levels (Investigating,
Implementing, Innovating). Passages marked [graphic] were transcribed from the rendered page. Only table cells that
mention privacy or data protection are quoted.


Policy, Ethical, & Legal Considerations: Implementing
Source: PDF page 1 (table cell) [graphic]

[graphic] The district is establishing policies, reviewing ethical guidelines, and strengthening a legal
framework to address the challenges associated with AI technologies, including student privacy,
data protection, and responsible AI practices. There is consideration of how AI can be used to
support all student populations, aiming to address equity gaps.

Policy, Ethical, & Legal Considerations: Innovating
Source: PDF page 1 (table cell) [graphic]

[graphic] The district has adopted Board-approved policies, robust ethical guidelines, and a strong
legal framework, demonstrating a commitment to accountability, data privacy, compliance, and
continuous improvement in AI. The district has a plan to evaluate the impact of AI, including efforts
to narrow educational equity gaps.

Student Use: Innovating (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1 (table cell) [graphic]

[graphic] Most students utilize AI to support their learning goals, critically assessing AI’s societal
impact, including biases, privacy concerns, and fairness issues, while making informed judgments
about the authenticity and origin of content.
