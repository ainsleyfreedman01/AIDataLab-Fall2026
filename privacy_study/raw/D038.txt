Board Vice Chair statement on data privacy (excerpt)
Source: page 1

“We believe that the policy provides clear direction on developing a committee to continuously
monitor and audit our systems to ensure data privacy, training staff and students on the responsible
use of AI for compliance to strict guidelines, and putting in place all needed and appropriate
parameters to ensure that AI enhances student learning and prepares them for a world in which AI
will continue to play a significant role.”
