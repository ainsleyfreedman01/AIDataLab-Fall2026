Board Policy CQD (LOCAL) (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

Any use of AI must comply with law, policy, and administrative regulations relating to student and
employee privacy and data security.

Dear Colleague Letter principles (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

In her Dear Colleague Letter on the use of artificial intelligence in education, Secretary Linda
McMahon outlined five principles for the responsible use of AI: education technologies should be
educator-led, ethical, accessible, transparent, and protective of student data.

Operational Use: Employee Use
Source: PDF page 2

District employees may use AI tools to enhance instructional planning, professional responsibilities,
and operational efficiency, while maintaining student privacy and confidentiality and complying with
District policy. Employees remain responsible for all instructional decisions, the accuracy of
AI-assisted content, and the protection of confidential or personally identifiable student information.
