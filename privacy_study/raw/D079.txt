For Students: Responsible Use (excerpt)
Source: web page

To ensure safe and ethical use, students should:

• Prioritize privacy: Never share personal information with AI models and be aware that user data
  may be used to train these models.

Original Draft: Using AI Responsibly: The Big Picture (excerpt)
Source: web page, original draft

• Privacy: Never share personal information (like your name, address, or grades) with AI tools.
  Think of everything you put into AI as potentially public.
