 Grant Parish Employee AUP and AI Addendum:
      Data and Student Privacy Passages
Excerpted verbatim from Employee Acceptable Use of Technology Resources Agreement and Artificial Intelligence
Addendum (Grant Parish School Board, Louisiana). Page numbers match the document's own page numbering. Ellipses
(…) mark omitted text.


Access and Use of Content/Resources (excerpt)
Source: pages 1–2

• Employees shall not store any personal information (e.g. personal pictures, personal files, etc.)
  on any district-owned computer or server.
• All files stored on the network shall be treated as district property subject to control and
  inspection by authorized district personnel.
• Email communication is not a confidential medium for transmitting information and should be
  viewed as public in nature. An IT system administrator may review communications to maintain
  integrity system-wide and ensure that staff members are using the system responsibly.
• Employee shall not allow others to have access to or use his/her email account.
• Employee email account shall not be readily accessible by others.

Illegal Activities (excerpt)
Source: page 2

• Employee shall not attempt to gain unauthorized access to or “hack” into the district’s network,
  any computer system through the district’s network, or go beyond employee authorized access.
  This includes attempting to access, alter and/or delete another person’s account or files.
• Cyberbullying can include sharing personal or private information about someone else causing
  embarrassment or humiliation.

Personal Privacy
Source: page 3

• Employee shall have no expectation of privacy in their use of GPSB’s technology resources.
  Employee use of the technology resources will be monitored, accessed, reviewed and retained.
  Illegal activity will be reported to appropriate authorities.
• Files or programs stored on a computer, server, or external drive that are in violation of this
  agreement may be removed by an IT system administrator without notice.
• Employee’s network activity and/or data stored on the district’s system including but not limited to
  a district computer, is a matter of public record.

Security (excerpt)
Source: page 3

• Employee shall be responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of passwords. Under no
  condition should the employee give his/her password to others nor post the password in written
  form to be viewed by others. Likewise, using another person’s credentials to gain access is not
  permitted.
• Employee shall not allow others to have access to or use his/her accounts including, but not
  limited to, computer login credentials, email, district software accounts.
• Employee shall log off or lock user account anytime the device is not in use to prevent
  unauthorized access.
• Employee shall immediately notify district technology department authorities if a possible security
  problem is detected. Employee shall not search for security problems, attempt to circumvent
  security safeguards or in any way gain unauthorized access to any system or files.

Security (excerpt, continued)
Source: page 4

• Employee shall not use technology resources for unauthorized disclosure of personal or student
  information protected by Family Education Rights and Privacy Act and/or La. Rev. Stat. Ann.
  §17:3914, for inappropriate contact with an employee or student, or for use and/or dissemination
  of personal identification or confidential information regarding any student or employee. With
  proper permission from the Superintendent or his/her designee, an employee may send selected
  information in an encrypted form.
• Employee activity and files residing on GPSB’s technology resources will be treated as district
  property subject to control, inspection, and/or search by an IT system administrator.

District-Owned Mobile Devices (excerpt)
Source: page 4

• An IT system administrator has the right to monitor, track, and document the overall activity of
  the device. This includes, but is not limited to, auditing the computer for usage, software
  installed, etc.

Artificial Intelligence Addendum: A.3 Privacy and Data Protection
Source: page 5

Users must protect the privacy of students, employees, and others when using AI tools:

• Do not enter student personally identifiable information (PII) into AI tools that have not been
  approved by the District
• Do not enter confidential employee information, IEP/504 data, health records, discipline records,
  or login credentials into unapproved AI tools
• Use only District-approved AI tools for work involving student data or official District operations
• Comply with FERPA, COPPA, and Louisiana student privacy law (La. R.S. 17:3914) in all AI
  interactions

Artificial Intelligence Addendum: A.4 No Expectation of Privacy and
A.5 Monitoring and Enforcement
Source: page 6

Users have no expectation of privacy when using District networks, devices, accounts, or systems,
including when interacting with AI tools. The District reserves the right to monitor, log, and review
activity on District technology resources to ensure compliance with this policy and applicable law.

The District may block or restrict AI tools that are unsafe, misused, inappropriate, or not approved
for District use.

Artificial Intelligence Addendum: A.7 Staff-Specific Expectations and
A.8 Policy Reference
Source: page 6

Staff must use only District-approved AI tools for instructional and operational work involving
student data. Staff may not independently contract for, purchase, or require students to use AI tools
that have not been approved through the District's review process.

For complete requirements regarding AI use, data privacy, the tool approval process, vendor
obligations, academic integrity, and consequences, see the District's Artificial Intelligence Use
Policy (Board Policy - AI) and the Administrative Procedures for AI Implementation.
