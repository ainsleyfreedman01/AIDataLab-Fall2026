Policy Statement (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

This policy establishes the standards for the instructional use of AI within the District to ensure such
tools remain educator-directed, maintain student data privacy, and respect parental rights. It is the
policy of the Bixby Public Schools to allow the use of AI tools in district operations and classrooms
under conditions that safeguard student data, protect academic integrity, and ensure equitable
access to educational opportunities.

All use of AI within the district shall comply with FERPA, COPPA, Oklahoma cybersecurity
standards, and OSDE AI Guidance.

Data Privacy and Security
Source: PDF page 1

The District shall adhere to the following data protection standards:

• Compliance: All AI tools must comply with federal laws, including the Family Educational Rights
  and Privacy Act (FERPA), and state student data privacy and security laws.
• Data Minimization: The District will take reasonable steps to minimize the amount of student
  data shared with AI tools.
• Prohibited Inputs: Consistent with District data privacy protocols, employees are strictly
  prohibited from inputting student identifiers (names, IDs, or other PII) into open AI systems (e.g.,
  ChatGPT) that are not formally vetted and contractually protected under FERPA.

Transparency and Data Disclosure
Source: PDF pages 1–2

• The District shall maintain transparency with families regarding AI use.
• At least annually, the District will provide parents and legal guardians with a written disclosure
  identifying:
• All AI tools currently in use within the District.
• The vendors providing those AI tools.
• The categories of student data collected and the extent of data sharing.
• The specific educational purpose for which each tool is used.
