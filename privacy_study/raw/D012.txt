  Ascension Parish Policy IFBGB, AI Acceptable
    Use: Data and Student Privacy Passages
Excerpted verbatim from Policy IFBGB, Employee and Student Acceptable Use Policy for Artificial Intelligence (Ascension
Parish School Board, Louisiana). Page numbers refer to the PDF pages of the source file. Ellipses (…) mark omitted text.


Access and Permissions (excerpt)
Source: page 1

• A list of district-approved Al tools will be maintained by the Information Technology Department
  and reviewed regularly. Staff may request an evaluation of additional tools by following the
  current software request procedures.

Privacy, Security, and Data Protection
Source: page 2

• Users must be aware of data privacy concerns with AI tools, especially regarding the handling of
  personal and sensitive information.
• Personally identifiable information (PII), confidential information, and/or sensitive information
  should never be shared with an AI tool unless such sharing is explicitly approved by the School
  Board.
• All Al tools must comply with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the
  Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and other relevant privacy laws.
• The Information Technology Department ensures that approved Al tools have robust security
  measures to protect user data from unauthorized access.
• Users are expected to be aware of and comply with the terms and conditions of all Al tools,
  specifically with respect to age requirements.
• The Information Technology Department conducts regular security assessments of
  District-approved AI technologies to safeguard against vulnerabilities.

Student and Parent Education (excerpt)
Source: page 3

The School Board will provide students and parents with resources to understand Al tools, their
appropriate use, and related risks.

Monitoring and Enforcement
Source: page 3

• The School Board reserves the right to monitor the use of AI tools to ensure compliance with this
  policy.
• This may include real-time monitoring of network activity and/or maintaining a log of Internet
  activity for later review.
• Users should have no expectation of privacy when using School Board technology or networks.
  All activity may be monitored to ensure compliance with this policy.
