Common AI Chatbots and Their Status (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'Chatbots Are AI Tools'

Common AI chatbots and their current status for ASD20 students include:

• ChatGPT: Blocked and not approved for ASD20 student use.
• Microsoft Copilot: Not approved for ASD20 student use.
• Google Gemini: Approved only for ASD20 high school students using the district enterprise
  version.
• MagicSchool AI Learning Assistant: Approved for ASD20 students at all grade levels when a
  teacher provides access.

How D20 Developed Its Approach (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'D20's Approach to AI'

• AI should be used ethically, legally and in accordance with District 20 policies, with appropriate
  data protection.

Recommended Tools (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'D20's Approach to AI'

MagicSchool is the district-recommended AI tool approved for students at all grade levels. …
Teachers can view every student interaction with MagicSchool.

Google Gemini was approved for students in D20 high schools beginning in August 2026. Students
must use the district enterprise version by signing in with their D20 account.

D20 carefully reviews approved digital tools, including tools with AI features, to help protect student
data privacy.

ChatGPT and ASD20 Students (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'D20's Approach to AI'

ChatGPT is blocked and is not approved for ASD20 student use. OpenAI's current terms allow
users age 13 and older, with parent or legal guardian permission required for users under 18.
Meeting those age requirements does not constitute district approval.

Accessing Google Gemini (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'D20's Approach to AI'

Students in grades 9–12 may use the enterprise version of Google Gemini. This is the
district-approved version and provides data protections through the district account.

Accessing MagicSchool (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'D20's Approach to AI'

Students can access only the digital rooms their teachers provide. Teachers control which tools are
available in each room.

Teachers can view every interaction between students and MagicSchool.

Risks Associated with AI (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'AI Literacy and Student Use'

AI risks include:

• Safety and data privacy concerns.

Protect Personal Information
Source: web page, section 'AI Literacy and Student Use'

Many AI apps may use, share or train their systems on user data. Students should use only
D20-approved enterprise tools that include district-required data protections.

Some apps, including AI companions, may pose significant risks to children and adolescents.
