Nemo Vista School District Policy 4.64: Data and
          Student Privacy Passages
Excerpted verbatim from Policy 4.64, Student Use of Artificial Intelligence (Nemo Vista School District, Arkansas; adopted
July 2025). Page numbers refer to the PDF pages of the source file. Ellipses (…) mark omitted text.


Selection of AI Tools (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

The District shall establish a committee who shall be responsible for the review and selection of AI
tools that shall be available for students to use. All AI tools selected by the committee shall comply
with applicable state and federal data privacy laws. Students shall only use AI tools that were
approved by the District committee for classroom assignments or projects.

Responsible Use: Protecting Personal Information (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

As part of using AI tools responsibly, students should be cautious about the information they provide
to AI tools and consult with a teacher if unsure. At no time shall Students share personal, sensitive,
or confidential information with AI tools, especially AI tools requiring data input.

Prohibited Actions: System Security (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

The following actions regarding AI tools are strictly prohibited:

• Using AI tools to:
• Gain unauthorized access to District systems; or
• Bypass District security measures;
Students should report any inappropriate content or security concerns encountered while using AI
tools to a teacher or administrator.
