I. Guiding Principles for AI Use in Education: Ensure Safety, Ethics,
and Effectiveness
Source: page 1 [graphic]

[graphic] Data privacy, security and content appropriateness should be primary considerations when
adopting new technology. Consider existing policies related to these issues and how the use of AI
fits into existing frameworks.

II. Learning with AI (excerpt)
Source: page 2

It is important for teachers to consider this question, along with the questions listed below, when
selecting an AI tool or website to use with students:

• Is the application age appropriate?
• How is data collected and used, and does it meet data practices standard?

II. Learning with AI: Opportunities and Challenges Table, Data Privacy
and Security (excerpt)
Source: page 3 [graphic]

[graphic] Data Privacy and Security: Ethical concerns surround student data collection, storage, and
use, requiring robust privacy policies and safeguards. (See Minn. Stat.13.32 [2026] Educational
Data)

III. Creating District-level Guidance: Begin with What Already Exists

IV. AI in Education Resources: Minnesota Resources (excerpt)
Source: page 5

• Minnesota Department of Transportation General Artificial Intelligence Standard: Clear guidance
  to ensure generative AI is used in alignment with the department’s core values, legal
  requirements, policies, and security practices.
• Minnesota IT Services Transparent Artificial Intelligence Governance Alliance: Guidance for
  using AI to improve government services while ensuring ethical, equitable, and secure practices.
