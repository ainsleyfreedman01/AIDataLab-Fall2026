Privacy & Security
Source: page 5

To safeguard student privacy, ensure instructional integrity, and boost professional productivity, please
follow these guidelines for district-approved AI tools (accessible via your district login). Do not use
non-approved AI resources for any work-related activities.
Student Privacy & Data Protection (FERPA Compliance)
In accordance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and PSJA ISD Board Policy
FL (LEGAL), safeguarding student data is a critical legal requirement.
Under no circumstances should staff enter, upload, paste, or describe identifiable student information
into public AI tools or prompts. Treat prompts as data leaving district systems. Even partial information
combined with context can make a student identifiable.
Examples of Strictly Prohibited Information:

• Basic Identifiers: Student names (first and last), Student ID numbers, dates of birth, home
   addresses, or parent/guardian names.

• Protected Records: Social Security numbers, disciplinary records, health/medical information, or
   human resources/payroll info.

• Academic & Support Data: Grades connected to an identifiable student, or Special Education (IEP)
   and Section 504 details tied to a specific student.
Incident Reporting Protocol
If you suspect a data exposure, a security breach, or the misuse of an AI tool, you must report it
immediately to mitigate data risk.

• Report to: PSJA ISD IT Helpdesk
• Submission Channels: Helpdesk Portal Link | helpdesk@psjaisd.us

Staff AI Expectations: Staff Security Protocols
Source: page 6

To ensure network security and data loss prevention, all staff members must adhere to the following
operational guardrails:

• District-Managed Accounts Only: Always log in using official PSJA ISD Microsoft 365 or Google
   Workspace credentials. Never use personal emails or accounts for district business.

• Approved Storage Systems: Store and share district documents only within approved
   environments (Google Drive, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams). Follow all existing sharing and
   permission rules.

• No Unapproved Add-ons: Do not install AI browser extensions, add-ons, or connectors (for Google
   Workspace or Office 365) without explicit IT administrative approval.

• Third-Party App Restriction: Staff are prohibited from authorizing third-party apps or granting AI
   tools permission to access district accounts and data sets.

• Managed Endpoints Only: If an authorized AI tool requires file uploads or document access, it must
   only be accessed on managed district devices equipped with active endpoint protection.

• Basic Identifiers: Passwords, access tokens, or any login credentials for district-managed systems.
