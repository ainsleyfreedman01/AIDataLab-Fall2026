[graphic] Equity and Accessibility for All: Staff and the District are committed to ensuring equity and

[graphic] Privacy and Ethical Use: Staff and the District are committed to maintaining privacy and

Student Rights: The Right to Privacy and Ethical Use
Source: PDF page 3

• Students have the right to protect their personal data and creative works from unauthorized AI
  training or exploitation while using district-provided AI tools.
• Students have the right to learn how to protect their personal information, which emphasizes
  ethical AI use.

Staff/District Rights: Privacy and Ethical Use (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 5

• Staff have the right to data privacy and control over how AI interacts with their personal and
  professional information in the same way as any other district-provided tool.

Staff/District Responsibilities (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 6

• Staff will only use district-approved AI tools, ensuring the protection of personal information
• Staff will avoid the use of AI tools that have not been vetted and approved by the District to
  protect student privacy and guard against bias.
• The District will maintain systems addressing emerging AI-related challenges, including, but not
  limited to, student misuse, data privacy concerns, and maintaining academic integrity.
