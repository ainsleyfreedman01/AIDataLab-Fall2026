Data Privacy and Security
Source: web page, section 'Data Privacy and Security'

Protecting the privacy and security of student and staff data is a non-negotiable requirement for AI
tool usage in RCSD, mandated by NYS Education Law 2-D and district regulation. Compliance with
federal laws such as the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the Children's Online
Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the Children's Internet Protection Act (CIPA) is also essential.
The District Data Protection Officer will oversee compliance in this area.

When considering or using AI tools, pay close attention to:
• Personally Identifiable Information (PII): NEVER input student or staff PII unless the tool is
  district-approved with a compliant data privacy agreement in place.
• Data Collection and Usage: Understand exactly what data is collected (PII, usage data, etc.)
  and ensure its use is clearly defined and limited to educational purposes.
• Data Security Measures: Approved tools must use strong security (encryption, access controls)
  to protect data.
• Compliance with Regulations: Verify that the tool explicitly complies with NYS Education Law
  2-D, FERPA, COPPA, and CIPA.
• Parental Consent and Control: Understand the tool's process for parental consent and their
  ability to review or delete a child's data (especially for students under 13).
• Transparency: Privacy policies must be clear and accessible.
• Data Retention and Deletion: Be aware of data retention periods and secure deletion
  procedures.
Report any data privacy concerns immediately to the Data Privacy Officer.
