III. Guidelines for AI Use (excerpt)
Source: web page, Board Policy IFBG (3)

A. Legal compliance: Employees must comply with all applicable laws and regulations, including
copyright, intellectual property rights, and data protection laws.

E. Privacy and data security: Employees must respect the privacy and data security of individuals
when using generative AI. It is prohibited to provide personally identifiable information of students or
staff to generative AI tools. It is also prohibited to use generative AI to create content that invades
the privacy or confidentiality of individuals, or to use data obtained through generative AI in a way
that violates applicable privacy laws.

IV. Permissible Use of AI Guidelines (excerpt)
Source: web page, Board Policy IFBG (3)

BHCS permits the use of district approved Artificial Intelligence. The School District is responsible
for the review, approval, and ongoing management of a list of permissible AI tools.
