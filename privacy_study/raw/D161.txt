Ethical Principles (excerpt)
Source: Google Doc, section 'Ethical Principles'

• Privacy and Legal Protection: Adhering to stringent data protection standards and federal law
  (FERPA, CIPA, COPPA, GDPR, DMCA)

Guidelines for Responsible AI Use (excerpt)
Source: Google Doc, section 'Guidelines for Responsible AI Use'

• Protect Student Information: Personally Identifiable Information should not be shared with AI
  tools

Roles & Responsibilities (excerpt)
Source: Google Doc, section 'Roles & Responsibilities'

District and School Commitment
• Monitor AI use to promote responsible implementation, protect privacy, and ensure compliance
  with district AI guidance
Teacher & Staff Commitment
• Ensure privacy by removing all personally identifiable information before using AI (names,
  Individualized Education Programs [IEPs], birthdays, assessments, etc.)
Student Commitment
• Develop skills to use AI responsibly, recognizing that all inputs into generative AI may be
  retained by the AI platform

Review & Implementation Process (excerpt)
Source: Google Doc, section 'Review & Implementation Process'

All AI tools undergo a multitiered AWE (Apps, Websites, Extensions) review process, which
includes:

• Privacy considerations and age restrictions
