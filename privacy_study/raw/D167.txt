Taliaferro County School District AI Use Policies
  (Draft): Data and Student Privacy Passages
Excerpted verbatim from AI Use Policies for Educators and Students (Draft March 2024, Taliaferro County School District,
Georgia). The source has no page numbers; locations are given by section. Ellipses (…) mark omitted text. The source is a
web page containing two draft policies, one for educators and one for students.


AI Use Policy for K-12 Educators: Policy Statement (excerpt)
Source: web page, 'AI Policy for Educators'

This policy outlines the acceptable and unacceptable uses of AI in our K-12 educational
environment. Our goal is to harness AI's potential to enhance educational outcomes while
safeguarding our students' privacy, security, and well-being.

Inappropriate Uses of AI in Education: Violating Privacy and Data
Security
Source: web page, 'AI Policy for Educators'

AI must not be used to collect, store, or analyze student data without explicit consent and a clear
educational purpose. It is inappropriate to use AI tools that infringe on students' privacy rights or fail
to comply with data protection laws (e.g., FERPA, COPPA).

Educators: Conclusion (excerpt)
Source: web page, 'AI Policy for Educators'

AI presents significant opportunities for enhancing K-12 education but must be used with careful
consideration to ethical, privacy, and educational standards.

AI Use Policy for Students: Purpose (excerpt)
Source: web page, 'AI Policy for Students'

The aim is to ensure that students engage with AI technologies responsibly, ethically, and effectively
to enhance their learning experiences while safeguarding their privacy and well-being.

Responsible AI Use: Ethical Conduct (excerpt)
Source: web page, 'AI Policy for Students'

Students are expected to use AI technologies ethically, respecting copyright laws, privacy norms,
and the intellectual property rights of others.

Responsible AI Use: Privacy and Data Protection
Source: web page, 'AI Policy for Students'

Students must be cautious when interacting with AI tools that require personal information. Sharing
sensitive or personal data without proper authorization or oversight is prohibited. Students should
understand the risks and implications of data sharing and seek guidance from teachers when
necessary.

Responsible AI Use: Safety and Security (excerpt)
Source: web page, 'AI Policy for Students'

They should immediately report any security breaches, suspicious activities, or exposure to
inappropriate content encountered during AI use to school authorities.

Monitoring and Compliance (excerpt)
Source: web page, 'AI Policy for Students'

The school will monitor the use of AI technologies to ensure compliance with this policy. Monitoring
will be conducted in an ethical manner, respecting the privacy and rights of students.
