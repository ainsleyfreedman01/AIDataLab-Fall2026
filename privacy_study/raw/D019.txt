Privacy, Security, and Confidentiality
Source: page 5

When using GenAI tools, it is crucial to understand that any information provided whether through
prompts entered by the user or AI-generated responses could potentially be used by companies to
train their models. This includes personal details of students, families, employees, and any
proprietary or confidential information belonging to Bentonville Schools. Assume that all information
shared with a GenAI application will be used to train the model and could become accessible in the
public domain. To safeguard privacy and maintain confidentiality, you should:

• Never input personally identifiable information or protected health information into GenAI tools.
• Never input confidential, sensitive or legally protected information into GenAI tools. Legally
  protected information would include, though is not limited to, information contained in a student’s
  record; information that identifies students, employees, and other individuals; information
  contained in an employee’s record; proprietary information; etc.
• Never input copyrighted material or proprietary Bentonville Schools intellectual property into
  GenAI tools.

Guidance for Students: 4.64 Student Use of Artificial Intelligence
Source: pages 6–7

All AI tools selected by the committee shall comply with applicable state and federal data privacy
laws. Students may only use AI tools in the classroom when specifically authorized by their teacher.
When students use their District user accounts, they are strictly limited to District-approved, vetted
tools to maintain legal compliance and student safety.

As part of using AI tools responsibly, students should be cautious about the information they provide
to AI tools and consult with a teacher if unsure. At no time shall Students share personal, sensitive,
or confidential information with AI tools, especially AI tools requiring data input. Students are
prohibited from using any AI tools that collect and employ user data to train or improve their
underlying models. The training of learning models is strictly limited to a classroom setting when it is
part of the curriculum instruction on model training, and the model must be managed by the teacher
(not utilizing external public data or services).

The following actions regarding AI tools are strictly prohibited:

• Altering or falsifying academic documents or records using AI tools;
• Using AI tools to: Gain unauthorized access to District systems; or Bypass District security
  measures;
Students should report any inappropriate content or security concerns encountered while using AI
tools to a teacher or administrator.

Guidance for Licensed Personnel: 3.58 Licensed Personnel Use of
Artificial Intelligence
Source: pages 8–9

• Only AI tools that have been thoroughly vetted and officially approved via the district’s application
  approval request ticket process are authorized for use. To submit a new tool for approval, please
  open a Helpdesk ticket at helpdesk.bentonvillek12.org;
• The use of AI tools by licensed employees shall adhere to District policy; State and Federal law;
  State rules; and federal regulations governing data privacy; and
While AI tools may be used to assist with administrative tasks, such as lesson planning, scheduling,
data analysis, and managing student records, final decisions impacting students or employees must
involve human oversight.

Employees are responsible for their use of AI tools and for safeguarding sensitive information.
Employees shall report any security incidents or potential data breaches immediately to a
supervisor or the IT department.

The failure to comply with this policy or a District policy governing the release of information may
result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

Guidance for Classified Personnel: 8.49 Classified Personnel Use of
Artificial Intelligence
Source: pages 10–11

The District will provide training on the use of AI tools, including data privacy requirements. All
classified employees shall participate in the District’s training on AI tools before they may use AI
tools to assist with any job duties. Classified employees shall participate in additional training
sessions and are expected to stay informed about changes to this and related policies regarding the
use of AI and data privacy and security.
