    Tacoma Public Schools Artificial Intelligence:
        Data and Student Privacy Passages
Excerpted verbatim from Artificial Intelligence (Tacoma Public Schools, Washington, district web page). The source has no
page numbers; locations are given by section. This is a district web page rather than a formal policy. Ellipses (…) mark
omitted text.


What is Microsoft Copilot? (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'What is Microsoft Copilot?'

The following safeguards are built into the chatbot by Microsoft:

• Age-based filtering for inappropriate content
• Privacy protections aligned with Microsoft’s education standards
• No data sharing with advertisers or third parties

What is Colleague AI? (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'What is Colleague AI?'

The tool actively monitors for safety by filtering for prompts like self-harm, abuse, and threats.
Notifications are sent to teachers and school counselors when inappropriate content is detected and
flagged.

How has the district prepared for using AI? (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'How has the district prepared for using AI?'

These efforts ensure AI is used to enhance learning while protecting student safety, privacy, and
academic integrity.

What district policies and regulations support AI use? (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'What district policies and regulations support AI use?'

Policy 6300 and 6300R: Emphasizes data security and responsible technology use, including AI.
