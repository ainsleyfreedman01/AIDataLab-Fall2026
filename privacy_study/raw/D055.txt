5. AI Governance
Source: page 3

The governance of all Artificial Intelligence (AI) platforms, tools, and services within the District shall
be the responsibility of the District Technology Review Committee (TRC). This committee serves as
the sole body authorized to review, evaluate, approve, and monitor AI technologies for use by
students, staff, or on district-owned accounts, networks, and devices.

All AI systems—whether instructional, administrative, or operational—must undergo the district’s
established technology vetting process before deployment. Approval will be granted only if the AI
system:

• Meets Children’s Internet Protection Act (CIPA), Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act
  (COPPA), Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), Health Insurance Portability and
  Accountability Act (HIPAA), Protection of Pupil Rights Amendment (PPRA), The Alabama Data
  Breach Notification Act of 2018, Alabama data-governance requirements, and district policy.
• Demonstrates appropriateness for K-12 educational use.
• Aligns with district instructional goals and security standards.
Approvals must be documented and reviewed annually. Vendors seeking to provide AI solutions to
the district must supply clear evidence of compliance with these standards prior to consideration.

All AI platforms, tools, and services—instructional, administrative, or operational—must be vetted
and approved by the District Technology Review Committee prior to any use by students or staff
and prior to connection to district accounts, networks, or devices. The use of any AI platform not
reviewed and approved by the District Technology Review Committee is strictly prohibited.
Unauthorized use may result in access restrictions, reporting to administration, and disciplinary
action in accordance with district policy.

The District Technology Review Committee will conduct periodic audits to ensure only approved AI
platforms are in use and will provide regular updates to district leadership on emerging AI
technologies and any recommended guideline changes. Approval is documented, time-limited, and
reviewed annually for compliance, performance, and risk.

Vendors must provide model/system cards or equivalent documentation describing intended use,
limitations, data flows, content safeguards for minors, and human-in-the-loop controls to support
educator oversight.

6. Data Privacy and Security
Source: pages 3–4

The Dothan City School District is committed to complying with federal, state, and local laws, rules,
and regulations, and other entities that provide standards for compliance to ensure data privacy and
security are maintained for data used in AI systems. Compliance includes FERPA, COPPA (for
students under 13), IDEA/Section 504 confidentiality, applicable Alabama data-governance
requirements, and district policy. We will ensure that the Contractor’s AI systems comply with all
applicable federal, state, and local laws, rules, and regulations, and other entities that provide
standards for compliance.

The Dothan City School District is dedicated to ensuring data to be used in the AI systems will meet
the data quality standards established by our Data Governance Policy.

The Dothan City School District will conduct compliance audits that will be done by our Technology
Department and/or the Data Governance Committee (DGC). The Technology Department and/or
the Data Governance Committee will submit a copy of the audit to our leadership and the respective
Contractor(s). We will also ensure that the Contractor(s) provide a Corrective Action Plan (CAP)
based on the findings in the audit report that will include the steps the Contractor(s) plan to take to
mitigate the findings, and the timeline by which they will be mitigated. Vendors must attest to
reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards aligned to recognized frameworks
(e.g., NIST) and provide breach notification within 72 hours of discovery to the district’s designated
contact.

The Dothan City School District will include in our procurement and contract document(s) verbiage
(Appendix A), which the Contractor shall attest when submitting their proposals and any contract
executed to perform the work. There shall be no use of district data for training new products without
prior written DCS approval and documented scope. No AI vendor may access district data without
an executed DCS Data Processing & Security Agreement and adherence to district data
minimization and deletion schedules.

12. Use of AI Systems (excerpt)
Source: page 6

In alignment with Dothan City Schools’ commitment to student safety, data privacy, and instructional
quality, the use of AI systems by students, staff, and all other district users shall be limited to those
platforms that:

• Are purpose-built for K-12 and minors, with age-appropriate safeguards and educator controls.
• Have successfully completed the District Technology Review Committee’s vetting and approval
  process, including compliance with all applicable laws, district policies, and data privacy
  standards.
• Have completed and returned the DCS Data Processing & Security Agreement

Appendix A: Data Usage Restriction Clause
Source: page 8

The Contractor hereby attests and agrees that it is expressly prohibited from using any data
provided to it by the data providers—either in its raw form or after undergoing any form of
processing, aggregation, or transformation—for the purpose of training a new AI System to be used
in a new product, model, service, or offering (collectively referred to as the "Product") of the
Contractor. The Contractor further attests and agrees that any violation of this clause will be
considered a material breach of this RFP and/or Contract to be entered into by the Contractor, and
may result in termination of the Contract, legal action, or both, at the sole discretion of the Dothan
City School District.

Notwithstanding the restrictions set forth in Section 1(a) of this Contract, the Contractor may use the
data provided by the data providers to:

• Customize the AI System currently in use, which may result in the development of a new AI
  System.
• Develop a new AI System.
The customization and development of the AI System is expressly required by the Dothan City
School District to meet the specific needs of the School District (the "District-Specific Needs"). Any
such use of the data for the district-specific needs shall be subject to prior written approval by the
Dothan City School District and shall be carried out in accordance with all applicable laws,
regulations, and the terms and conditions of this contract.

Appendix A: Request for Data Use and Model Training Completion
Source: page 9

Details of Data Use: The RFA shall include a comprehensive written explanation that outlines the
following, but is not limited to:

• Purpose of data usage.
• Data elements that will be used for the AI System training.
• Duration of data usage.
• The manner the data providers’ data will be stored, processed, and secured.
• Name(s) of the individual(s) who will have access to the data.
• The controls in place to prevent unauthorized use.
• Specific types of data that will be used for model training.
• Risks associated with using the data for model training and how these risks will be mitigated.
