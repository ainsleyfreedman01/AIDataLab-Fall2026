Current Regulations Relevant to AI in Education
Source: Google Doc, Section 5: ABC’s Plan for AI Use in the Classroom

United States
FERPA - AI systems must protect the privacy of student education records and comply with parental
consent requirements. Data must remain within the direct control of the educational institution.

COPPA - AI chatbots, personalized learning platforms, and other technologies collecting personal
information and user data on children under 13 must require parental consent.

CIPA - Schools must ensure AI content filters align with CIPA protections against harmful content.
