AI Tools Available for CCS Students (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'What AI tools are available for CCS Students?'

Currently, there are no AI tools approved in the CCS Trusted Learning Environment (TLE) for
student use. Our primary focus is on comprehensive staff training and ethical review to ensure that
when we introduce AI to students, it is integrated effectively, safely, and in alignment with our
educational goals.

Ethical and Appropriate Use of AI for Staff
Source: web page, section 'What does ethical and appropriate use of AI look like for our staff?'

Ethical and appropriate AI use enhances instruction, streamlines operations, and maintains privacy
while keeping the educator at the center of the learning environment.

Ethical uses include:
• Maintaining data privacy
• Using CCS approved AI tools
• Avoiding use of Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
Appropriate uses include (excerpt):
• Analyzing data, including no personal identifiable information
