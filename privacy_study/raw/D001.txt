Educator use of AI (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'Educator use of AI'

Educators may only use AI applications, programs, or tools that have been approved by 27J. …

Educators must comply with all applicable federal, state, and local student privacy laws, including
the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Colorado Student Data
Transparency Act, as well as with 27J policies and procedures regarding internet and technology
use (GBEE), staff use of AI (GBEE-R2), and privacy and protection of confidential student
information (JRCB).
