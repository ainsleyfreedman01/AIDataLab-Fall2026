Appendix G: Glossary of Terms: LearnPlatform
Source: page 12

The Learn Platform is a website designed to support educators and other stakeholders in knowing
which apps, websites, and extensions have been reviewed for use in Canyons School District.
Technology tools will be approved through Learn Platform if they follow FERPA, CIPA, and COPPA.
