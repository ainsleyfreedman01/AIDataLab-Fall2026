Selection of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools (excerpt)
Source: web page

AI tools will be vetted by the superintendent or superintendent's designee. Decisions to use AI tools
should be focused on:

• Privacy: Personally Identifiable Information (PII) of students will not be shared without prior
  written consent, where the third-party vendor with access to PII qualifies under the School Official
  exemption established FERPA and described in policy 712.
• Transparent and Interruptible: Student use of AI tools must be able to be monitored by
  licensed staff to safeguard the appropriateness of the learning experience for the student and
  monitor for accuracy of the AI tool.
