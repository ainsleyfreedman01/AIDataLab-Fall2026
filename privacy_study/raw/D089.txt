AI Literacy: With AI Literacy, I CAN (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1 [graphic]

• [graphic] Assess safety and privacy with apps and data sharing
• [graphic] Collect data responsibly

Security
Source: PDF page 4 [graphic]

[graphic] AI tools are powerful but can be exploited by threat actors to gain information for a
strategic advantage. Consider the information to which you are providing access through your
queries.

• [graphic] Ensure inputs into public-facing AI tools are free from personal identifiable information
  (PII).
• [graphic] Create a use policy to address best practices when making decisions about AI
  implementation.
• [graphic] Follow local policies regarding student data. Ensure compliance with FERPA/COPPA
  before using AI with students.
• [graphic] Ensure students are using AI tools approved for their age range.
• [graphic] Develop a “sandbox” where educators can practice making decisions about security
  issues related to AI.
• [graphic] Engage parents and caregivers in learning how to talk to children of all ages about AI
  safety.

Key Considerations for AI Policy: Process Guidance (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 6 [graphic]

• [graphic] Determine the hierarchy of decisions related to AI, and provide clarity about what
  should be considered when selecting AI supports designed to enhance student learning,
  especially requirements related to data privacy, security, and compliance with existing laws
  (FERPA, COPPA, etc.).
