Excerpted verbatim from South Carolina Artificial Intelligence (AI) Standards Framework (South Carolina Department of
Education; copy hosted in the state-ai-policy-field-guide repository). Page numbers match the document's own page
numbering. This is a 15-page computing/AI course standards framework for students (not a district use policy), so its
privacy content appears as student learning standards. Text marked [graphic] was transcribed from table cells whose
text-layer extraction interleaves columns; it was checked against the PDF's table structure. Ellipses (…) mark omitted text.

Standard 11, Security and Privacy
Source: page 15 (table rows) [graphic]

[graphic] Students will research security and privacy protocols associated with AI and ML solutions
and apply the protocols to protect the data throughout the processes.

Row 1
• [graphic] Level 1: Identify AI and ethics-related laws and analyze their effect on digital privacy,
  security, intellectual property, network access, contracts, and harassment.
• [graphic] Level 2: Discuss security and privacy issues that relate to AI applications.
• [graphic] Level 3: Research the security vulnerabilities of machine learning models and ways to
  mitigate them.
• [graphic] Level 4: Research and evaluate the security issues relevant to machine learning model
  creation, storage, and usage.
Row 2
• [graphic] Level 1: Explain the ethical methods of collecting data from human subjects while
  protecting privacy and security for use in training machine learning models.
• [graphic] Level 2: Evaluate the issues related to privacy and security in the development and use
  of AI and ML models.
• [graphic] Level 3: Evaluate methods of direct and indirect AI interaction, such as using
  AI-enabled systems, contributing data to a dataset, and trading privacy and security for access to
  an AI tool.
• [graphic] Level 4: Apply data protection protocols within AI and ML solutions, addressing
  concerns throughout the data cycle-collection, storage and use of iterative processing
  algorithms.
• [graphic] Level 2 (second item in cell): Research the ethical, security, and privacy issues that
  affect data gathering, storage and overall data quality that can contribute to bias in a model.
