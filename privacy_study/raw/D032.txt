Technology 7100: Definitions (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'Technology - 7100'

COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act): A law created to protect the privacy of children
under 13.

CIPA (Children's Internet Protection Act): A law requiring K-12 schools and libraries in the United
States to use Internet filters and implement other measures to protect children from harmful online
content as a condition for federal funding.

Student Resource Access: Accounts, Approved Apps and AI
Platforms (Section 1)
Source: web page, section 'Student Resource Access - Section 1'

Every student will be provided a username and password, providing access to Google Services.

A list of District approved Apps for Google, Apple, and Chromebook devices are available for review
at the educational technology department.

Schoology is the District’s Learning Management System … All students will have an account with a
username and password. Internal communication will be managed to allow students and guardians
to communicate with the classroom teacher but not with other members of the system.

The District permits students to use approved Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools to enhance learning
and practice skills, not to replace the student’s own effort or critical thinking. All AI tools must be
age-appropriate and used under the guidance of a teacher. To protect student privacy, only AI tools
that have been officially vetted and approved, which guarantee that student data is not used to train
the AI mode and comply with all applicable privacy laws (e.g. FERPA, COPPA) may be used.

Filtering and Monitoring: Expectation of Privacy (Section 2)
Source: web page, section 'Filtering and Monitoring - Section 2'

At any time and without prior notice, Campbell County School District (CCSD) reserves the right to
monitor, inspect, copy, review, and store any and all usage of technology devices, digital resources,
and network infrastructure, along with information technology and any information sent or received
in connection with this usage. Staff and students should not have any expectation of privacy
regarding such materials.

Filtering and Monitoring: Monitoring (Section 2)
Source: web page, section 'Filtering and Monitoring - Section 2'

The District monitors the use of its network to protect the integrity and optimal operation of all
computer and system networks. There is no expectation of privacy related to information stored and
transmitted over the District network. The information on the network in general files and email is not
private and is subject to review.

Technicians and computer system administrators have full access rights to the storage devices they
manage as part of their duties. Routine maintenance and monitoring of the system could lead to the
discovery that a user has or is violating District policies, state laws, or federal laws. Search of
particular files of a user could be conducted if there was reasonable suspicion that a user has
violated the law or Campbell County School District policies.

Technology Acceptable Use: Digital Citizenship (excerpt, Section 3)
Source: web page, section 'Technology Acceptable Use - Section 3'

• Student users will not reveal personal addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal
  information, for themselves or others.
• Staff users will not reveal personal addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal
  information, for students, or colleagues.
• Users will not give their username or password to any other person, or use the username or
  password of someone else to access any part of the system.

Student Images and Products for Educational Use (Section 3)
Source: web page, section 'Technology Acceptable Use - Section 3'

The District through the use of technology provides students with enriching and innovative
opportunities. As the audience we communicate with expands, students may have the opportunity
to have their photograph, video, recording, and/or student digital projects shared or published on
websites and other media, or through the District.

Unless otherwise noted in Student Policy 5147-Student Records, the following will apply:

• No portrait style photographs or photographs containing personal identifiers will be allowed.
• Students’ last names are never published.
• Students’ home addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers are never published.

Student Use of Electronic and Recording Devices 5330: Expectation
of Privacy
Source: web page, section 'Student Use of Electronic and Recording Devices - 5330'

Taking photos or making audio or video recordings without permission in any context in which the
person has a reasonable expectation of privacy such as private offices, restrooms, changing rooms,
labs, classrooms, and conference rooms is prohibited. Only persons authorized by the building
administrator will be allowed to photograph or record in restricted areas for specific purposes. These
persons might include media or yearbook photojournalists.

It is also not acceptable to publish pictures or recordings taken in an inappropriate situation or
generated using AI or other electronic platforms. In other words, a picture or recording taken under
prohibited conditions is also prohibited from being published. This includes pictures taken off district
property and brought to the district for distribution. Transmission on the Internet constitutes
publication. … The use of electronic devices to transmit or record images or conversations without
