Introduction (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

Any use of artificial intelligence technology in the Board’s educational programs or operations must
be in accordance with State and Federal law as well as Board policies, including but not limited to
the following: … Policy 8330 – Student Records; Policy 7540.03 – Student Technology Acceptable
Use and Safety; and Policy 7540.04 – Staff Technology Acceptable Use and Safety.

General Principles: Ethical Use (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

All members of the … community must use AI technologies in ways consistent with institutional
values, privacy standards, FERPA, copyright laws, and the integrity of employees and students.

Employee Use of AI (excerpt)
Source: PDF pages 2–3

Employees shall not input sensitive or proprietary information about students, colleagues, or
institutional operations into AI systems that lack safeguards and policies to protect such data from
being used in their training models, and if such sensitive or proprietary information will be entered
into an AI system, employees shall seek the approval of their Principal before doing so.

Employees shall not enter sensitive information, such as personally identifiable data or confidential
institutional documents, into AI systems. If such information must be used in an AI system, staff
must seek their supervisor’s approval when doing so.
