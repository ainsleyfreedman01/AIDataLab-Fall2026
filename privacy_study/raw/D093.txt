Guidance for Educators: Tool Review (excerpt)
Source: web page, section "Guidance for Students & Educators"

Jeffco educators use AI tools to make learning more personalized, engaging and effective while
prioritizing student safety and learning.

We follow a rigorous review process to ensure that every AI tool meets our standards for security,
accessibility and educational value. Each tool is evaluated for its purpose, age appropriateness,
ease of use, technical needs and impact on student privacy.

FAQ: What AI tools does Jeffco support? (excerpt)
Source: web page, FAQ

Jeffco-supported tools for students include:

• AI applications that do not collect personal student information (such as email, name, or
  birthdate) and meet age requirements, typically 13 and older.
