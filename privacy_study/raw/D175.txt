Sharing Your Personal Data
Source: page 2

To access and use most AI tools, you have to create an account. Before sharing your personal
information and creating an account, students and parents are advised to carefully read the
product’s Terms of Use policy.

Examples of Appropriate and Inappropriate Use of AI Tools (excerpt)
Source: page 2

Students must have device permissions to accept AI tools on a school issued device and/or have
permission from their parent/guardian.
