Legal Compliance and Approved Tools (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

Any use of GenAI in schools, on school networks or devices, or for school purposes must be
compliant with federal, state, and local law. Any use of Gen AI must consider adherence with
Education Law 2-d and its regulations. Staff may only use software, subscriptions and Gen AI tools
that have been vetted and approved by the District.

Sensitive or Other Confidential Information
Source: PDF page 2

Information input into GenAI tools that have not been approved by the District may be used for
algorithm training and made available to other users of the system outside the district. District users
are strictly prohibited from inputting personally identifiable information (PII) into any Gen AI tool that
has not been approved by the District; sensitive, copyrighted, proprietary, or confidential district
information; or any information regulated by applicable federal, state, and local law and district
policies. These include but are not limited to the following:

• Student information protected by FERPA (see policy 5500) or Education Law 2-d (see policy
  8635).
• Staff information protected by New York State Education Law 2-d (see policy 8635), or other
  confidential personnel information.
• School district information that would be withheld from a Freedom of Information Law (FOIL)
  request (see policy 1120).
• District-owned copyrighted works (see policy 8650)
