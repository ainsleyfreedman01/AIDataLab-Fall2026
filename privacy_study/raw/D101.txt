Excerpted verbatim from Policy 5430, Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence (Loudoun County Public Schools, Virginia;
draft clean copy, C&I Committee May 7, 2025). Page numbers match the document's own page numbering. Ellipses (…)
mark omitted text. The source PDF prints a line number beside every line; these were removed when quoting.


B. General Policy on Generative Artificial Intelligence
Source: page 1

Only approved Generative AI programs may be utilized in student instruction or in completing
student work.

C. Monitoring and Review: Annual Review
Source: page 1

Generative AI programs approved for use by staff or students will be reviewed annually to ensure
compliance with Division policies and state and federal student data privacy laws.

D. Student Use of Generative AI (excerpt)
Source: page 2

To ensure Generative AI is used in ways that support their learning, students will commit to:

• Practicing good digital citizenship habits, respecting privacy and online safety, and using
  Generative AI technologies responsibly for learning and collaboration.

D. Teacher Use of Generative AI (excerpt)
Source: page 2

To ensure Generative AI is used in ways that support safe learning, teachers will commit to:

• Protecting the privacy of personally identifiable information about themselves or their students
  with any Generative AI tool.

D. Staff Use of Generative AI (excerpt)
Source: page 3

To ensure Generative AI is used in ways that support operation efficiency, staff will commit to:

• Using Generative AI in ways that protect the privacy of staff, students, and families.
• Being mindful of the use of Generative AI in ways that protect Division information and uphold
  applicable laws and policies.

D. Prohibited Use of Generative AI
Source: page 3

• Using Generative AI tools that are not provided and/or authorized by the school Division.
• Sharing personally identifiable information with Generative AI programs.
