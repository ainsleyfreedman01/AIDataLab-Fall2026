Guidelines for Use: All Users (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

1. Before using any AI tool with students, employees must verify that the tool has been approved by
the District and meets District data privacy and student safety standards.

2. AI tool evaluation must include: openness of the environment, student data privacy compliance,
equitable access, potential for inherent bias, and accuracy safeguards.

3. All AI use must comply with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the Student
Online Personal Information Protection Act (SOPIPA), and all applicable District policies.

4. Users may not share confidential information, student records, or personally identifiable
information with any AI system.

Staff Use (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

12. Final grades and evaluative decisions must reflect human professional judgment. AI-generated
grades or scores may not be applied to student records without human review and approval by a
certificated employee.

Prohibited Uses (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

All students, staff, and users are prohibited from using AI systems to:

• Share confidential, personally identifiable, or sensitive student or staff information.
• Assign final grades, generate official student records, or make high-stakes educational decisions
  without direct human review and professional judgment.
• Use platforms or tools that have not been vetted and approved by the District.

Approval and Vetting
Source: PDF page 2

The Superintendent, in coordination with the Chief Technology Officer, shall maintain a list of
approved AI tools and a vetting process consistent with the criteria established in this policy. This
list shall be reviewed and updated no less than annually.
