Approved Software (excerpt)
Source: web page, Overview

MPS takes the use of AI seriously. Use of AI systems and tools must be safe and effective for
students and staff. Reference the MPS Approved Software list to see which AI tools have officially
been approved by MPS.

Note that not all tools are approved for everyone. For example, some tools may only be used at the
high school level, while other tools may only be used by staff.

Laws and Acts AI Tools Must Follow
Source: web page, Overview

Use of AI systems and tools must be safe and effective for students and staff. Additionally, we must
adhere to the following laws/acts:

FERPA - Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act:
Protects the privacy of student education records & gives parents certain rights regarding student
education records

AI systems must protect the privacy of student education records and comply with any parental
consent requirements.

Data must remain within the direct control of MPS.

COPPA - Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act:
Imposes requirements on websites and online services directed to children under 13 years of age,
or that collect personal information from a child under 13.

AI chatbots, personalized learning platforms, and other technologies collecting personal information
and user data on children under 13 must require parental consent.

Troubleshooting: Tools Not Approved, Blocked Tools, and PII
Source: web page, Troubleshooting

The tool/site I want to use isn't approved by MPS
MPS staff may request software by following the process outlined on MPS Digital Resource
Standards Site .

I can't open ChatGTP
ChatGTP is not allowed for use at MPS. It does not meet MPS data privacy and security standards.
As a result, the ChatGTP website is blocked.

What's PII?
PII stands for personal identifiable information. Working in education means that there are several
data privacy laws which apply to us and our use of AI tools. Reference our Best Practices guide for
examples of PII.
