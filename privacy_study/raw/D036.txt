Is AI safe to use in the classroom?
Source: web page, FAQ 'Is AI safe to use in the classroom?'

When used responsibly, Artificial Intelligence (AI) can be a safe and valuable tool for learning. Our
district only allows the use of AI systems that meet strict safety, privacy, and educational standards.
Additionally, all approved resource providers are required to sign our DPA (Data Privacy
Agreement). Any AI tools used in our classrooms must comply with federal and state student
privacy laws, including:

• FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act): protects the privacy of student
  education records
• COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act): applies to online services used by
  children under 13
• Texas student data privacy laws: provides additional safeguards around how student data is
  collected and used

What AI does my child have access to? (excerpt)
Source: web page, FAQ 'What AI does my child have access to?'

Our district provides students with access to carefully selected, school-approved tools that include
Artificial Intelligence features to support learning. … In addition, some of the digital tools our
students already use include built-in AI features, such as writing support, feedback, or tutoring
assistance. These features are part of platforms that have been reviewed and approved by the
district.

What policies are in place for AI use? (excerpt)
Source: web page, FAQ 'What policies are in place for AI use?'

All guardians must agree annually to the Celina ISD Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) as defined in
CQ(Local). The following Artificial Intelligence clarification is included in the AUP:

… Students, teachers, and staff members must ensure that any AI systems they use are
age-appropriate and that they do not violate the privacy of other individuals. …
