     Cherry Creek AI Guidance: Data and Student
                 Privacy Passages
Excerpted verbatim from Artificial Intelligence (AI) Guidance (Cherry Creek School District, Colorado). The source has no
page numbers; locations are given by section. Ellipses (…) mark omitted text. This is a web page with audience tabs
(elementary, middle, high school, educators, administration, support staff, families), FAQs, and a policy list.


Guiding Principles (excerpt)
Source: web page, introduction

Keeping student and staff information safe builds trust and creates a secure environment where
learning can thrive. When people feel protected, they are more willing to explore, think, and grow.

Guidance for AI Use: Elementary
Source: web page, tab 'Elementary'

Safe
Your privacy and safety come first when using AI.

Never type your name, school, or personal information into AI tools. The tools you use at school are
chosen to help keep your information safe.

Guidance for AI Use: Middle School
Source: web page, tab 'Middle School'

Safe
Protect yourself and your data online.

Think carefully about what personal information you share with AI. Only use AI tools approved by
the school and follow guidelines to keep yourself and others safe.

Guidance for AI Use: High School
Source: web page, tab 'High School'

Safe
Be aware of privacy, security, and digital ethics.

Use AI in ways that respect data privacy, school policies and legal standards, digital ethics, and
your own well-being. Use AI responsibly and be aware of overreliance on AI.

Guidance for AI Use: Educators
Source: web page, tab 'Educators'

Safe
Protect student privacy and wellbeing at all times.

Use only district-approved AI tools and never upload student personally identifiable or sensitive
information into external systems. Protect your own well-being by using AI responsibly and be
aware of overreliance on AI.

Guidance for AI Use: School Administration
Source: web page, tab 'School Administration'

Safe
Safeguard privacy, security, and student wellbeing at all times.

Ensure only district-approved AI tools are used and that all privacy, data protection, and security
requirements are followed. Promptly address any concerns related to misuse, bias, or breaches.

Guidance for AI Use: District Support & Operations Staff
Source: web page, tab 'District Support & Operations Staff'

Safe
Protect data privacy and uphold security requirements at all times.

Use only district-approved AI tools and never enter staff or student personal or sensitive information
into external or non-vetted systems. Safeguarding data supports the wellbeing and trust of the
Cherry Creek community.

Guidance for AI Use: Families
Source: web page, tab 'Families'

Safe
Your child’s safety and privacy are a top priority.

The district reviews AI tools for security, privacy, and fairness before use. Remind your child not to
share personal information with AI systems at home or at school.

FAQs (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'FAQs'

Which AI tools can CCSD employees use?
CCSD employees should only use AI tools if they’re district-provided or have been approved
through the district’s review process. When in doubt, don’t use it with student or staff data and ask
your school/district tech support team.

Which AI tools can high school students use?
CCSD high school students should only use AI tools that are district-provided and have been
approved through the district's review process.

Policy: What guides AI use (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'Policy: What guides AI use'

Existing Board policies already outline expectations for equity, academic integrity, and student data
privacy as they relate to AI use.

JRCB – Student Data Privacy (Adopted 8/11/25)
AI tools must pass a privacy review before use with student information.
