Guiding Principles for AI Use: Approved Tools Only (excerpt)
Source: page 1

• Approved Tools Only: Only district-approved AI tools may be used. All tools undergo a District
  Approval Process to ensure they meet safety, security, age-appropriateness, and instructional
  standards.
• A regularly updated list of approved AI tools, including any usage/age restrictions, is maintained
  on … the Instructional Resource Dashboard on the CCSD SharePoint site for staff to reference
  and access.
• Students may use AI-enabled tools only in accordance with district policy, legal requirements,
  and … based on the tool’s age restrictions.

Security, Privacy, and Safety
Source: pages 1–2

CCSD will implement safeguards to ensure the integrity, safety, and ethical operation of all AI
systems used across the district. Protecting personal and confidential information remains a top
priority. CCSD safeguards include district review and approval of AI tools, configuration of platform
features when possible, alignment to Board policy, and ongoing review as technologies evolve. Staff
are expected to use only district-approved tools and features enabled by CCSD.

• Prohibited Data Entry: Staff and students must not input personally identifiable information (PII)
  or confidential data into AI platforms (CCSD School Board Policy IFBG). Prohibited Data
  includes Any combination of data that could reasonably identify an individual, such as):
• Student and/or staff names and initials
• Student and/or staff ID numbers
• Dates of Birth
• Assessment scores or performance data
• Medical, IEP or ESOL information
• Images, videos or audio recordings of students

Responsible Academic Use of AI (excerpt)
Source: pages 2–3

• Approved Tools Only: Utilize software tools that leverage AI only if vetted and approved
  through the CCSD Instructional Resource Approval Process.
• Data Privacy: Never input confidential or personally identifiable information into AI platforms.
• Self-Assessment Questions: These are questions that you should ask each time you generate
  a prompt or … use an AI tool.
• Could this prompt reveal the identity of a student or staff member?
• Am I entering information considered confidential in other contexts?
• Would I be comfortable sharing this information publicly?
