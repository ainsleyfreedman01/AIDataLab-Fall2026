Policy (excerpt)
Source: pages 1–2

LAUSD is committed to utilizing new AI technologies, in particular, generative AI, in an ethical,
transparent, and responsible manner, while recognizing the importance of protecting student and
employee privacy and ensuring that the use of these technologies is consistent with ethical and
equitable considerations.

In developing the District’s AI policy, LAUSD prioritizes the safety and privacy of its students and
staff as it seeks to aid and support them with modern technologies. Staff and students must be
proactive about using … AI and ensuring the protection of confidential data and information in
compliance with federal and state confidentiality laws.

Background (footnote 1, excerpt)
Source: page 2

In accordance with the District’s Responsible Use Policy, students under the age of 13 are not
permitted to use generative AI tools.

Initial Guidance from the U.S. Department of Education (excerpt)
Source: page 3

Emphasize Humans in the Loop. … Employees, students, and families must exercise control over
AI software used in the school context in a hands-on method that ensures integrity and
confidentiality of student and district information.

Align AI Models to a Shared Vision for Education. … Emphasizing data privacy and minimizing
bias must be prioritized when using AI software.

Design Using Modern Learning Principles. … Modern learning principles encourage human
regulation of any data provided to AI software …

Considerations Prior to Use of AI
Source: page 4

Prior to use, adoption, or purchase of any AI tool, District staff should ensure that the AI tool has
been approved by the District for data privacy, including any applicable confidentiality laws or
regulations that may apply to the data; and security requirements. AI tools must be vetted by the
following offices prior to approved use:

• Procurement Services
• Information Technology Services

Data Privacy and Security
Source: page 5

The District and individual District Users must ensure confidential, sensitive, and privileged
information is protected when using AI. District users shall not share any confidential, sensitive,
privileged or private information when using, prompting, or communicating with any AI tools. As
described in LAUSD BUL-1077, Sensitive Information includes, but is not limited to, pupil records,
employee personnel records, and health records, and such Sensitive Information requires additional
safeguards.

District Users of AI tools must follow these data privacy and security guidelines:

• Prioritize protecting your own and others’ confidential and sensitive information, especially
  students’ personal data.
• Do not share your own or others’ confidential, sensitive, or private information in AI prompts,
  including but not limited to pupil or employee record information or images, videos, or voice
  recordings. Bear in mind that AI tools generally take whatever users enter into a prompt and
  incorporate it into their systems/knowledge base for other uses.
• Do not share non-public information and protected information without approval from the Office
  of the General Counsel (OGC), the Office of Data and Accountability (ODA), and Procurement
  Services Division (PSD). See BUL-1077.2 regarding the District’s information protection policy
  and BUL-6887.1 regarding LAUSD’s pupil records confidentiality policy.
• Ensure that AI results used in District Information Systems are reviewed for any imbedded
  malware.
• District Users must comply with all applicable IT data security and data retention policies and
  procedures.
