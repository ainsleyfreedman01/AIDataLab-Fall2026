3. Privacy, Data Security, and Safety
Source: web page, section 3

We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and data security of our educators and learners. We
adhere to all applicable data protection regulations and implement robust security measures to
prevent unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of personal information. When utilizing AI
technologies, data collection and processing data will be anonymized or pseudonymized whenever
possible. We prioritize data minimization, ensuring that only the necessary information is collected
and stored.

Enterprise-Grade Protections: As a Google Workspace for Education Plus district, our version of
Gemini comes with additional, enterprise-grade safety protections. This means our students and
staff use a service designed specifically for a school environment, which is separate and more
secure than the publicly available consumer version.

District Content Filtering: In addition to Google's safeguards, all access to Gemini on our network
is routed through the district's own content filters. This adds a critical layer of protection, aligning
with our existing internet safety policies and providing an even more secure experience for our
students.

Our goal is not just to provide access, but to teach responsible and ethical use, reinforcing the
principles of digital citizenship in the age of AI.
