Executive Summary: The Challenge (excerpt)
Source: page 2

Continued inaction risks student misuse, over-reliance on AI, harm to cognitive and social-emotional
development, data privacy violations, workforce unpreparedness, and uneven educational
experiences across 1,200+ districts.

Executive Summary: Recommendation 2, Issue Statewide Guidance
(excerpt)
Source: page 2

Texas should publish guidance covering responsible use policies for both educators and students,
data privacy, AI procurement and student assessment.
