What is AI? (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'What is AI?'

Importantly, the AI tools used in MNPS are carefully reviewed and approved to make sure tool are
safe, private, and age-appropriate. We do not allow AI tools that interact with or collect personal
student information without going through the proper approval process.

Guiding Principles: Privacy, Data Protection, and Security
Source: web page, section 'Guiding Principles'

Protecting personal and sensitive information is crucial, complying with applicable local, state, and
federal laws to ensure student and staff data privacy and security.

Guiding Principles: Transparency (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'Guiding Principles'

The district shall ensure that AI systems used by students and/or employees, as well as those
embedded within vendor products, are understandable with clear explanations of how decisions are
made, fostering trust and accountability.

Community Notification and Approved Tool List (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'Community Notification'

In accordance with MNPS Policy 4.124, the Director of Schools will provide notice to parents and
guardians regarding the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) programs in the district. A current and
approved list of AI tools is published and maintained on the district website to ensure transparency
and alignment with our commitment to responsible technology use.
