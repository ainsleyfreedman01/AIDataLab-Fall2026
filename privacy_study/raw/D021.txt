Principle 2: Privacy & Security
Source: page 1

Privacy & Security: AI usage will comply with regulations that protect student data privacy, safety,
and accessibility. WVBE Policy 2460 requires that counties are responsible for ensuring that
COPPA, CIPA, and FERPA are not violated.
