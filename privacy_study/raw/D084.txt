Purpose (excerpt)
Source: web page, Purpose

Our genAI implementation plan ensures that artificial intelligence is understood and used legally,
ethically, and in ways that elevate teaching, learning, and operations, while upholding our district's
standards for data privacy, security, inclusivity, and academic integrity.

Student Guidelines (K-5 and 6-12): Prohibited Uses (excerpt)
Source: web page, Student AI Guidelines & Curriculum

Generative AI tools may NOT be used in violation of terms of service, academic code of conduct, or
board policy.

Student Guidelines (K-5 and 6-12): Special Considerations
Source: web page, Student AI Guidelines & Curriculum

Data privacy and security: Students should not input any personally identifiable information into any
generative AI tools.

Teacher Guidelines: Prohibited Uses (excerpt)
Source: web page, Teacher AI Guidelines

Generative AI tools may NOT be used for the following purposes:

• Compromise Privacy/Security: Teachers should not use generative AI in ways that compromise
  teacher and/or student privacy/security. ( Policy 605.4 )
• Vetted Generative AI: Teachers should not use generative AI applications that have not been
  vetted by the district. ( Policy 605.4 , Policy 605.8R1 )

Software Evaluation Process
Source: web page, Software Evaluation Process

Before using any AI tool or software, staff should submit it through our district's evaluation process
to ensure alignment with goals, data privacy, and security standards.

AI Focus Areas: Strengthen Operational Systems (excerpt)
Source: web page, AI Focus Areas

Implement and monitor genAI tools to enhance decision-making, optimize resources, and improve
operations across the district, using our Software Evaluation Process to ensure alignment.

FAQ: How does our district protect student data privacy?
Source: web page, FAQ

Student data privacy is a top priority. All AI tools used by staff and students must go through
ICCSD's Software Evaluation Process before use. Students are instructed never to enter personally
identifiable information into any AI tool. Only district-approved and vetted AI applications may be
used, in accordance with Policies 605.4 and 605.8R1.
