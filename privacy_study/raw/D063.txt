   Falls Church City Public Schools Policy II/GF:
        Data and Student Privacy Passages
Excerpted verbatim from Policy II/GF Use of Artificial Intelligence (Falls Church City Public Schools, Virginia; adopted June
23, 2026). Page numbers match the document's own page numbering. Ellipses (…) mark omitted text.


I. Purpose and Scope (excerpt)
Source: page 1

The School Board also recognizes that the use of AI raises important questions about student
learning, privacy, equity, academic integrity, developmental readiness, and human connection.

III. Guiding Principles (excerpt)
Source: pages 1–2

The use of AI in FCCPS shall be human-centered, developmentally appropriate, transparent,
equity-aware, and protective of student privacy and well-being.

At the same time, AI use poses risks through inaccurate or biased outputs, overreliance,
inappropriate disclosure of student data, or substitution for work students are expected to do
independently of AI.

VII. Privacy and Data Security
Source: pages 3–4

FCCPS shall protect student privacy and data security in all AI use.

Personally identifiable student information, confidential records, disability-related information,
mental health information, and other sensitive student data shall not be entered into AI tools except
through approved division systems and in accordance with applicable privacy protections.

VIII. Communication and Learning (excerpt)
Source: page 4

The division shall provide students and staff with ongoing, age-appropriate learning about AI,
including its uses, limitations, ethical considerations, risks, academic integrity expectations, privacy
implications, and role in future study and work.
