Guiding Principles: We protect safety, privacy and data
confidentiality
Source: web page, section 'Guiding Principles'

We protect safety, privacy and data confidentiality. AI is one of many technologies used in our
schools, and its use aligns with existing privacy and data confidentiality policies. We understand the
risks of AI and take care to keep confidential student and employee data out of public models and to
secure the use of AI within our environment.

Responsible Use of AI (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'Responsible Use of AI'

Staff are encouraged to explore, learn, and use AI tools in accordance with existing laws, District
policies and guidelines. Each user bears the responsibility to ensure compliance with applicable
laws and policies regarding data security and privacy. AI users are also responsible for reviewing all
AI-generated output before use.

Age appropriateness
Source: web page, section 'Tips for Teachers / Tips for Students'

In accordance with Federal CIPA and COPPA regulations, most common consumer-grade GenAI
tools, such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini, are not suitable for use by students under age 13.
Education-specific GenAI tools may be appropriate for use by students under age 13, subject to
approval of the Chief Academic Officer and General Counsel, and/or their designees. … A list of
authorized applications and tools will be provided on the LBUSD website.
