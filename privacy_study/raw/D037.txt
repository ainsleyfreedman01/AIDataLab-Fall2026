What families should know (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'What families should know'

How will CCSD protect student data?
Any AI tool adopted by the district must meet data privacy and security standards consistent with
FERPA and district policy. Student information will not be used to train commercial AI models
without appropriate authorization.

Can AI be used to make decisions about my child?
No. District policy explicitly prohibits AI from serving as the sole basis for high-stakes decisions,
including discipline, special education placement, or academic advancement. Human judgment and
oversight are required in all such decisions.
