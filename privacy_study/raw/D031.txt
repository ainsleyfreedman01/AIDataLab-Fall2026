Data Security, Approval, Equity & Access
Source: web page, section 'Data Security, Approval, Equity & Access'

Student Data Privacy: Safeguarding CPS students' personal information is a top priority.
Generative AI collects data to inform its algorithms, so its use should be intentional and mindful of
privacy.

Adherence to Guidelines: Follow the guidelines of the Student Data Privacy Consortium (SDPC)
to ensure tools used comply with student data privacy agreements. Please refer to this CPS list of
approved tools.

Approved vs. Not Approved Tools: CPS-approved Generative AI tools include SchoolAI for all
students, Canva and MagicSchool for students 13 and older, and Gemini for CRLS students. Tools
like ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot are not approved for student use.

Confidential Data: Do not input student or staff personally identifiable information (ex. name, email,
ID, etc.) into open or public Gen AI models, even if using paid subscriptions like ChatGPT Plus or
MagicSchool.
