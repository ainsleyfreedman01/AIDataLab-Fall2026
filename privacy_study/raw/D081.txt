Training (excerpt)
Source: page 1

The Board delegates to the Superintendent the authority to:

• Remove access to the District’s computer systems and databases for noncompliance with
  training requirements as appropriate.

Use in District (excerpt)
Source: page 1

Any use of AI must comply with law, this policy, and administrative regulations outlined in the
District’s AI Guidebook relating to student and employee use, privacy, and data security.
