1. Guiding Principles (excerpt)
Source: PDF pages 1–2

A. The use of AI and emerging technologies must support SCPS’s educational mission and
operational integrity, and must comply with all applicable federal and state laws, including:

• Florida Information Protection Act (FIPA)
• Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)
• Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)
B. All technology use must prioritize:

• Protection of personally identifiable information (PII)

2. Acceptable Use (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

C. No confidential, proprietary, or sensitive data may be input into AI systems unless the system has
been reviewed and approved by the District’s Information Services Department.

3. Oversight and Compliance (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

A. The Superintendent or designee shall establish administrative procedures for:

• Evaluating and approving AI tools

Student Use of Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language
Processing Tools (excerpt)
Source: PDF pages 2–3

Teachers have the discretion to authorize students to use AI/NLP tools after providing explicit
instruction of the following:

A. When using AI/NLP tools students need to be aware that they are potentially sharing personal
data for possible inclusion in AI datasets.

G. Student access to certain websites/tools using AI may be granted. However, privacy guidelines
and age restrictions may be considered prior to allowing student access.

Use of AI by Teachers and Staff (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 3

C. AI/NLP use requires scrutiny to protect against the compromise of personally identifiable
information and/or violation of District policies/procedures.

E. Teachers/Staff should share neither passwords nor any other confidential, proprietary, or
sensitive District/student information with AI/NLP tools.

F. Student records, names, addresses, or other personally identifiable information shall not to be
uploaded to AI/NLP tools by teachers/staff.

G. AI/NLP tools may not be integrated with District software without the authorization of the District's
Information Services.
