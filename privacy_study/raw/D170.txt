What are Chromebooks? Cloud-Centric Storage and Enhanced
Security (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'What are Chromebooks?'

Cloud-Centric Storage:
Google provides 100 GB of cloud storage with each Chromebook, which ensures that files are
securely stored and accessible from any internet-connected device. This approach minimizes the
risk of data loss and simplifies access to work across different devices.

Enhanced Security:
Chrome OS is built with multiple layers of security, including automatic updates and sandboxing,
which isolates apps and processes to prevent malware from spreading. These features make
Chromebooks a secure option for handling sensitive information and performing online tasks.

Regular Updates:
Chrome OS is updated automatically, ensuring that users benefit from the latest features and
security improvements without needing to perform manual upgrades.

The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Education: Use of Student Data
(excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'Impact on Students' and 'Impact on Teachers'

Personalized Learning:
AI enables personalized learning experiences by analyzing individual student data to tailor
educational content and assessments to their unique needs and learning styles.

Data-Driven Insights:
AI tools can analyze large volumes of data to provide insights into student performance and learning
patterns.

Predictive Analytics:
AI can use predictive analytics to forecast student outcomes and identify potential issues before
they arise.
