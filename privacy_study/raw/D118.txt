   Moore Public Schools AI Instructional Use
 Guidelines: Data and Student Privacy Passages
Excerpted verbatim from MPS AI (Artificial Intelligence) Instructional Use Guidelines (Moore Public Schools). Page
numbers refer to the PDF pages of the source file.


Personal Safety and Privacy
Source: page 1

• Do not create accounts or encourage students to create accounts in any AI platforms without first
  making sure the platform is district approved.
• Protect not only your personal information and privacy but also that of others while using AI.
• Do not share sensitive personal information through a chatbot, such as full name, address,
  phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
• Be cautious when interacting with AI, and avoid disclosing confidential or sensitive information
  about yourself or others

Legal and Ethical Compliance (excerpt)
Source: page 1

• Do not use AI to engage in any form of illegal activity, including but not limited to hacking,
  spreading malware, phishing, fraud, or any other activity that violates the law.
