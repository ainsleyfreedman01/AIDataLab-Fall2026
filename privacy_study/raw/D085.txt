Data Privacy
Source: PDF page 1

The School is committed to protecting student and staff data consistent with state and federal laws
and regulations. The use of AI shall adhere to existing data privacy policies including, but not limited
to, policies related to the release of student records and information (see Policy 294).

Procurement of AI Tools
Source: PDF page 1

The adoption and use of AI tools shall be in accordance with the School’s procurement policies and
in alignment with the School’s mission. All AI technologies provided by vendors shall adhere to the
School’s policies and current state and federal laws and regulations.
