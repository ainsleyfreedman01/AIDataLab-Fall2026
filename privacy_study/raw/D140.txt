Principles: Secure and Private (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

The Governing Board recognizes the transformative potential of artificial intelligence (AI) …
Therefore, the Board is committed to supporting the use of AI by students and staff in accordance
with the following principles:

• Secure and Private: The district will prioritize security and privacy when changing existing
  practices or adopting new practices regarding AI.
