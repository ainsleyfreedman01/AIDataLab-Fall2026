Equity, Safety and Human Connection (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

Safety and security remain critical aspects of our AI strategy. We prioritize robust data privacy and
cybersecurity measures to protect student information and ensure safe digital environments. Ethical
considerations and transparency guide our use of AI, supported by rigorous human oversight,
ongoing evaluation and regular community discussions. This framework allows us to proactively
address privacy concerns, identify and correct algorithmic bias … and uphold principles of fairness.
