Purpose and Compliance with Data Privacy Law
Source: PDF page 1

The Cutter Morning Star School District Board of Directors desires to provide guidance for the
appropriate use of AI technologies in curriculum development, instruction, and related educational
activities that is aligned with the Arkansas Academic Standards and safeguards student data and
privacy while encouraging innovation.

Any AI tools or programs utilized by the District must adhere to State and federal laws; State rules;
and Federal regulations governing data use, privacy, and security. Any AI tool used must comply
with the District’s policies on data privacy and security, especially concerning student information.

Review and Approval of AI Tools
Source: PDF page 1

The superintendent shall establish a committee who shall be in charge of reviewing and approving
any AI tools, including automated decision tools, before they may be used. The committee shall
establish the procedures the committee shall use for approving AI tool selection.
