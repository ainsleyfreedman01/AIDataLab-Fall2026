I. Overview (excerpt)
Source: page 1

• Every family understands how AI is used in their child's education and trusts that student privacy
  and integrity are protected.

II. Definitions (excerpt)
Source: page 1

District-Approved AI Tools: AI applications that have been reviewed by the District AI Committee
and Technology Department, vetted for compliance with applicable privacy laws, and listed on the
district's approved application registry (SDPC).

Personally Identifiable Information (PII): Any data that could be used to identify a specific student or
staff member, including but not limited to names, student ID numbers, IEP contents, disability
status, grades, and family information.

III. Roles and Responsibilities (excerpt)
Source: page 2

Students are expected to:
• Use only district-approved AI tools for school-related work.
• Never enter personal information — their own or anyone else's — into any AI tool.
Educators are expected to:
• Use only district-approved AI tools in their professional practice and when introducing AI to
  students.
• Ensure students do not enter PII — including IEP contents, 504 Plan, individualized student data,
  or other confidential student/family information.

VII. Data Privacy and Security
Source: pages 4–5

The Manchester School District takes the protection of student and staff data seriously. All AI use
must comply with:

• FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act)
• COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
• NH RSA 189:66 (Data Inventories and Governance)
• NH RSA 189:68-a (Student Online Personal Information Protection)

Key Requirements
• Only district-approved AI tools — those listed on the district's SDPC registry and covered by a
  signed Student Data Privacy Agreement — may be used with students or for student-related
  work.
• No staff member may enter student PII, including IEP contents, grades, behavioral records,
  health information, or family data, into any AI tool unless that tool has been explicitly authorized
  by the District AI Committee and Technology Department to receive personally identifiable
  information.
• Staff must use district-managed accounts when accessing AI tools — personal accounts are not
  permitted for school-related AI use.
• The district maintains a current data governance plan, publicly available and reviewed annually,
  in compliance with NH RSA 189:66.
Tool Approval Process
Before any AI tool may be used in district classrooms or operations, it must be submitted to the
District AI Committee and Technology Department for review and approval. The review will assess
the tool against criteria including instructional alignment, data privacy compliance, accessibility,
accuracy, and environmental impact (including energy efficiency). A current list of approved tools is
maintained at the district's SDPC registry.

VIII. Community Engagement (excerpt)
Source: page 5

• Host AI information events to deepen the public’s understanding of how the responsible use of AI
  supports learning. These events should provide explanations of how AI is used in classrooms,
  what protections are in place, and how families can support responsible use at home.

IX. Professional Development (excerpt)
Source: page 6

• All staff will receive foundational AI literacy training, including an overview of this policy,
  academic integrity expectations, and data privacy requirements.

Appendix A: Student AI Use Agreement (excerpt)
Source: page 6

• Protect the privacy of myself and others — I will never enter personal information into an AI tool
  without express permission from my teacher.
