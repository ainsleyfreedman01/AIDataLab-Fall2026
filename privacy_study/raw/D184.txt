YSD1 and Artificial Intelligence (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'YSD1 and Artificial Intelligence'

Recognizing its value as an innovative educational tool, the Board is committed to its responsible
and ethical integration in accordance with district policies and state and federal laws, including
FERPA, CIPA, COPPA, and IDEA.

AI Utilization by District Educators (excerpt)
Source: web page, section 'AI Utilization by District Educators'

Source: web page, section 'Prohibited Use of AI in YSD1'
