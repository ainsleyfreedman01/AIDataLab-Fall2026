Purpose
Source: page 1

The Board's policy on Artificial Intelligence (AI) aims to ensure the ethical and responsible use of AI
technologies. This policy is designed to enhance teaching, learning and administrative processes
while safeguarding student privacy and data security.

It also complies with relevant state and federal laws, regulations, Board policies, administrative
regulations and school rules, including:

• Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).
• Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA).
• Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).
• Children’s Internet Protection Act (CIPA).
• Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Authority (excerpt)
Source: page 2

The district shall not be responsible for the dissemination, replication or alteration of information or
data input by any student or staff into any Generative AI tool or resource.

Delegation of Responsibility (excerpt)
Source: page 2

The Executive Director of Technology will continually evaluate and authorize Generative AI tools
and resources used in district schools and programs for age-appropriateness, bias, privacy
protections, accessibility standards, student and staff safety, and data security.

The Board directs that only district-authorized Generative AI tools and resources may be used on
district computers and in district schools and programs. Staff shall consult the district’s list of
authorized Generative AI tools and resources prior to implementation in the educational
environment. Unauthorized Generative AI tools and resources may not adhere to required data
privacy, monitoring and security standards.

The district solicitor, in coordination with the Executive Director of Technology, shall evaluate new
and existing vendor contracts, collective bargaining agreements, and related agreements for

Definitions: Open-source AI (excerpt)
Source: page 2

Open-source AI – AI tools and resources that are built on publicly accessible platforms and use and
share data among all users who access the platform, both within and outside of the district.

FERPA Compliance
Source: page 3

All teachers, administrators and staff are required to adhere strictly to the regulations outlined in the
Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) when utilizing any AI resources.

The district prohibits the use of personally identifiable information of students while using AI
websites, tools or applications that have not been pre-approved by the Executive Director of
Technology or his/her designee. The Executive Director of Technology shall establish an efficient
process by which teachers and administrators can request the approval of new AI resources. The
Executive Director of Technology or designee shall maintain a public list of all such approved
resources.

Ethical Considerations (excerpt)
Source: page 3

The district shall only authorize Generative AI systems and platforms appropriately equipped for
preventing breach of personally identifiable information and addressing the district’s prohibitions
against discrimination, harassment, bullying, bias and access to sexually explicit materials, or those
which are harmful to minors or prohibited by Board policy.

The district’s technology protection measures shall be enforced during use of Generative AI on
district computers and network resources.

Evaluation and Monitoring of Generative AI (excerpt)
Source: pages 4–5

Administrators, network supervisors and teaching staff shall establish processes for ongoing
evaluation and monitoring of Generative AI tools and resources used within the district and on …
district computers and network resources, including periodic assessments of the impact on student
learning.

Grading Student Work (excerpt)
Source: page 5

Teachers shall ensure that personally identifiable student records are kept secure when using such
grading technology.

Consequences for Inappropriate Use (excerpt)
Source: page 6

Students and staff must immediately report any violations or suspicious activity to the building
principal or designee.

Illegal use of Generative AI, intentional modification without permission or damage to files or data
belonging to others, copyright violations, and theft of services shall be reported to the appropriate
legal authorities for possible prosecution.
