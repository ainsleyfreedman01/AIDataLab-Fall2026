Excerpted verbatim from Guidance for the Use of Artificial Intelligence (Fayette County Public Schools, Kentucky; last
revised August 2024). Page numbers refer to the PDF pages of the source file. Ellipses (…) mark omitted text. The PDF's

Purpose (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

This guidance complements existing expectations and policies for technology use, data protection,
and academic integrity.

Guiding Principles for AI Use (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

The following principles guide the appropriate and safe use of AI and address current and future
educational goals, academic integrity, and security.

We will use AI as it aligns to educational goals. … We aim to reduce barriers to AI tools, while
safeguarding student information and privacy, to provide equitable access to this technology.

[graphic] We will ensure AI use aligns to existing policies and guidance. AI is one of many
technologies used in schools and it is developing at a rapid rate. All AI tools used should adhere to
state and federal privacy laws, such as the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the
Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and Kentucky House Bill 5 (2015). We will also
monitor AI usage to ensure it aligns with the district Acceptable Use Policy for both staff and
students, as well as guidance on academic integrity in the Student Code of Conduct.

… AI literacy instruction and support will include how to use AI to support learning, when to use it,
how it works, and how to evaluate tools for accuracy, bias, and information security.

Responsible Use of AI Tools: Student Learning (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 3

Foster Responsible Use Students will become discerning AI users, fostering responsible use
through evaluating outputs, understanding data security, and maintaining academic integrity.

Data Security Students will develop an understanding of how data is being used in order to foster
responsible data use and protect private information.

Responsible Use of AI Tools: Teacher Support (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 5

[graphic] Staff will review information entered in AI tools to ensure student privacy and protect
sensitive information.
