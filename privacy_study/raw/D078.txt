Student Responsibilities for Appropriate Use of AI (excerpt)
Source: page 2

Students are only permitted to use Generative AI systems listed in the Hingham Public Schools
SDPA Application Catalog. These systems are in compliance with federal guidelines regarding
student data privacy and will ensure that personal information is protected in line with our data
security policies.

Ethical Considerations: Privacy Concerns
Source: page 4

Using AI tools in educational settings requires strict adherence to student data privacy laws,
including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and Massachusetts student
record regulations (603 CMR 23.00). Public AI tools should never be used to input personally
identifiable student information unless the platform is explicitly approved by the district and includes
robust, legally compliant data protection measures.
