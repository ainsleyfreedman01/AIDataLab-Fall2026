Source: web page

Mustang Public Schools is committed to the responsible, transparent, and secure use of artificial
intelligence (AI) in support of teaching and learning.

Annual & Ongoing Review
Source: web page

The district annually reviews AI tools used in our schools and provides information regarding their
educational purpose, data privacy practices, and student protections. Parents and guardians may
review the current list of approved AI tools below.
