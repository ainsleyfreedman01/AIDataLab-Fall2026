Excerpted verbatim from AI in Action Across KY: Empowering educators and students through responsible,
human-centered AI integration (Kentucky Department of Education, December 2025). Page numbers refer to the PDF
pages of the source file. Ellipses (…) mark omitted text. This is a short summary brochure with little detailed privacy
content.


Updating the Kentucky AI Guidance Brief (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

Ongoing refinements to Kentucky’s AI Guidance Brief ensure that districts have current, practical
direction on safe, secure, and responsible AI adoption.

Statewide Data Collection to Monitor Intentional, Responsible AI Use
Source: PDF page 2

Kentucky gathers district- and student-level data to understand how AI is being used, encouraged,
and monitored across the state. These insights guide policy decisions and support targeted
improvements.

Kentucky’s AI strategy is rooted in the existing KETS framework, ensuring that adoption is grounded
in equity, security, interoperability, and sustainability.

Professional Learning: AI-Based Efficiency Tools (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 3

KDE models the use of AI-powered productivity tools that district staff can adopt to improve
efficiency and streamline administrative tasks while maintaining security and transparency

Early Warning Reporting Tool in Infinite Campus (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 3

Kentucky’s AI-enabled early warning system identifies students who may need support in
attendance, engagement, or academic persistence. This helps educators intervene earlier and more
effectively.

AI Driven Network Monitoring Tools That Strengthen Cybersecurity
Readiness
Source: PDF page 4

GoGuardian uses AI and machine learning to help keep students safe online by analyzing browsing
activity and on-screen content in real time. Its tools identify and block harmful or inappropriate
content, detect potential safety concerns such as self-harm or violence, and alert school staff so
adults can review the context and respond appropriately.

The AI is designed to support, not replace, human decision-making, helping create safer digital
learning experiences.
