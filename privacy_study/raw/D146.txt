What is the District's process for approving AI tools and other online
resources?
Source: web page, FAQ

A final determination will be made after all factors have been considered (i.e. terms and conditions,
data privacy agreement, existing tools, academic fit, cost, redundancy, interoperability, etc.).

What kinds of data should I avoid sharing with AI tools?
Source: web page, FAQ

You should be very careful not to share sensitive records with any AI system. Sensitive records may
include but are not limited to the following: Personally Identifiable Information,, sometime referred to
as PII (ex: name, address, birth date, etc), educational records (ex: IEPs, 504s, etc), student photos,
health records, usernames, passwords and proprietary information.

What guardrails are in place to make sure student and teacher
information is safe and not made public?
Source: web page, FAQ

Even though they may appear similar on the surface, the AI tools used in Plano ISD are
substantially different when compared to consumer (public) tools. This is especially true with regard
to privacy and data governance. The tools we offer have been designed to comply with privacy
standards, acting as a "school official" under FERPA.

Information entered into, uploaded to, or generated by approved tools through a District-provided
account is not used to train or improve AI models. It stays within Plano ISD’s digital perimeter. Chats
and content generated by our approved tools are not reviewed by humans outside of our
organization or, potentially, by the tool providers upon our explicit request. Prompts and responses
stay within our organization's security boundary. Student data is never used for advertising
purposes. These guardrails, coupled with AI Literacy basics, help keep your child's information safe.
