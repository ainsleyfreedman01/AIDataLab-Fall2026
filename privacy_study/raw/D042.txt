Section 1- Scope and Authorized Use (excerpt)
Source: page 3

Student AI use is permitted only when a staff member has expressly assigned an AI Use Level
(Appendix B) in writing; only on staff-approved platforms (Section 4); without input of personally
identifiable information; and in ways that reflect genuine student learning.

Section 4- AI Applications
Source: page 6

Platforms incorporated in any lesson must be on the district approved list. This list can be found at
www.chclc.org/apps . The AI Coordinator conducts a formal annual review of the approved list
before each school year. Platforms may be removed at any time for non-compliance with law,
policy, or student safety. Staff are notified of all list changes.

Section 5 – Prohibited Uses of AI (excerpt)
Source: page 6

The following are prohibited for all students regardless of device ownership or whether the student
is on or off school premises.

• Inputting the personally identifiable information of any student, staff member, or other individual
  into any AI platform
• Using AI tools not approved by the district
• Using AI to circumvent district technology security measures, access controls, or content filters

Section 11- AI Coordinator Oversight and Plan Review (excerpt)
Source: page 8

The Superintendent designates one or more AI Coordinator(s) responsible for: maintaining the
approved platforms list; processing tool approval requests; … tracking AI-related data; monitoring
applicable law and technology developments; and coordinating with the Director of Technology on
procurement, privacy, and cybersecurity.

Building Principals ensure building-level plan fidelity, communicate the plan to staff annually,
oversee parent notification and consent, and escalate concerns to the AI Coordinator.
