II. Artificial Intelligence: Student Privacy and Security
Source: web page, section II

Student Privacy and Security: AI used in schools will comply with all applicable laws, including the
Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act. All AI will be evaluated to maximize safe practices for
students while protecting from unlawful disclosure of student information and other risks to students
and staff.

II. Artificial Intelligence: Transparency (excerpt)
Source: web page, section II

Transparency: The use of AI will be transparent so that parents/guardians can understand and
become knowledgeable of the specific technology used in schools.
