Use of AI Tools by Licensed Employees (excerpt)
Source: PDF page 1

The use of AI tools by District licensed employees shall adhere to the following:

• Only those AI tools approved by the District’s committee for the selection of AI tools may be
  used;
• The use of AI tools by licensed employees shall adhere to District policy; State and Federal law;
  State rules; and federal regulations governing data privacy; and
• The use of AI tools shall operate in a manner that allows staff, students, and parents to have the
  opportunity to access information on how AI tools are integrated in teaching and learning within
  the district.

Safeguarding Sensitive Information and Reporting Breaches
(excerpt)
Source: PDF page 2

While AI tools, including automated decision tools, may be used to assist with administrative tasks,
such as lesson planning, scheduling, data analysis, and managing student records, final decisions
impacting students or employees must involve human oversight.

Employees are responsible for their use of AI tools and for safeguarding sensitive information.
Employees shall report any security incidents or potential data breaches immediately to a
supervisor or the IT department

The failure to comply with this policy or a District policy governing the release of information may
result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.
