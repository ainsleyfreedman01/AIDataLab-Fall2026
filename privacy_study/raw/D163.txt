Protecting Data Privacy
Source: page 17

Personally Identifiable Information (PII) must never be entered into any AI platform.

Prohibited Information (What NOT to Share):
• Identity: Names, addresses, phone numbers, SSNs, or birth dates.
• Educational Records: Grades, transcripts, IEPs, or disciplinary records.
• Health Information: Medical records, therapy notes, or allergy information.
• Financial Info: Family income or bank details.
• Photos or Videos: Images or recordings of students.
• Communication: Personal logs, emails, or student-teacher messages.
Best Practices for Anonymizing Data*
• Remove PII: Exclude all identifiers from prompts entirely.
• Generalize Information: Describe educational needs (e.g., "a student struggling with phonics")
  rather than specific student details.

• Use Hypothetical Scenarios: Create "mirror" scenarios that reflect a student's educational

Privacy Protocol: Minimal Disclosure
Even with strict safeguards, data fragments can sometimes be reassembled to identify an individual.
To mitigate this risk, adhere to the principle of data minimization. Share only the essential
information required for context and support; protecting privacy is most effective when you limit the
details provided.

Employees are required to complete annual training regarding FERPA (Family Educational Rights
and Privacy Act).

Questions regarding PII or FERPA should be directed to school adminstration.

MagicSchool Guidelines (excerpt)
Source: page 18

Spartanburg School District Four’s adopted AI platform for students is MagicSchool ONLY. Other
platforms utilized by students are in direct violation of Spartanburg School District Four’s Acceptable
Use Guide.

Age Restrictions: MagicSchool, an AI platform built for K-12, allows users of any age. Student users
are added to the platform and supervised by their teacher.

COPPA Compliance: MagicSchool AI and other tools must be used in accordance with the
Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act. Use for students under 13 requires strictly
district-approved, "built-for-education" models.

AI Literacy: Before any student is permitted to use MagicSchool AI, students will receive annual
instruction on AI literacy (including but not limited to fact-checking, formal/informal citations, and
data privacy protections) to understand the technology's limitations, ethical implications, and
Spartanburg School District Four’s policies.
