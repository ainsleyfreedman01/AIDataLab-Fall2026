Student privacy passage pilot

Purpose
Locate evidence about model training, secondary use/sharing, and retention/
deletion without treating keywords as automatic interpretations.
The topic-modeling script is separate and unchanged.

Requirements
R and pdftools. The first pilot used a session-local R library.
No policy text is sent to a remote service. The CSV policy corpus is not used.

Run from repository root
Rscript test_passage_pilot.R
Rscript passage_pilot.R "Privacy Passages" "/absolute/path/to/new-output" "/optional/R/library"
Rscript evaluate_passage_pilot.R "/absolute/path/to/new-output" .
Rscript compare_passage_rules.R "/absolute/path/to/new-output"

The extraction script refuses a nonempty output directory to protect reviews.
The scripts inventory all PDFs but retrieve/analyze only the ten-file pilot.
No OCR or silent substitution is used; extraction failures are explicit.
PDF page is the page in the excerpt compilation, not the original source page.
Source labels preserve the compilation's reported original location.
Raw pages and adjacent paragraphs support review of qualifiers and exceptions.
No source's legal status is inferred from the PDF filename or mandatory wording.

Review workflow
1. Open START_HERE.txt for the overview and quality decision, then
   quality_report.txt for initial frozen-rule results and limitations.
2. Read revision_v2_notes.txt and revised_rules_in_sample_quality.csv for the
   revised finder. These results reuse the old sample and are NOT validation.
3. Use candidate_evidence_v2.csv to inspect candidate text with context.
4. Have a teammate independently fill full_read_review_template.csv without
   looking at assistant labels or hit flags. yes/no/uncertain are relevance codes.
5. Compare to provisional_full_read_reference.csv, resolve disagreements, and
   revise the codebook with a version log. Keep original independent labels.
6. Check the PDF visually and original source for important quotations.
7. Evaluate frozen revised rules on fresh documents before full-corpus claims.
8. Split relevant paragraphs into individual provisions before substantive coding.

Reference review provenance
passage_review_documents.csv lists all ten excerpts fully read by the assistant.
passage_reference_labels.csv records positive/uncertain cases and selected
explicit negatives. All other paragraph/category pairs in those fully reviewed
documents are labeled no when building the expanded reference table.
This reference is provisional, not independently annotated human ground truth.
passage_provision_examples.csv provides illustrative assistant coding only.
It is not a complete substantive coding of all retrieved provisions.

Quality safeguards
No-hit categories are audited against the full-read reference.
Initial and revised rules remain distinct; original rule hash is verified.
Undefined precision/recall is left blank, never replaced with 100%.
Repeated paragraphs are flagged, not counted as additional documents.
Near-duplicate templates still need assessment before comparative statistics.
Original-source verification and independent annotation remain open.
